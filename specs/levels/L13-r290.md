# L13 — Intervention sur R290 (propane)

**Mission:** "Vitrine réfrigérée R290 en magasin : compresseur à remplacer. Intervention en sécurité."

**Skills:** A1 group 12 (12.01–12.14), 1.07, 1.08, 5.09, 11.03, 11.05 — new in A1 vs. old cat. I, so weighted heavily in the refresher path.

## Setup
Scene **R290**: plug-in display case, R290 charge on label (e.g. 150 g — IEC 60335-2-89 (2019, harmonised in the EU in 2023) raised the limit from 150 g to 13 × LFL ≈ 494 g per circuit; the game takes the label value), process tubes (no Schrader valves) on some variants, shop floor with customers, electrical sockets, a light switch, a radiator.

Tools: portable **HC gas detector** (continuous), ATEX-compatible recovery unit and **HC recovery cylinder** (red band / flammable label), vacuum pump suitable for A3, piercing pliers / line-tap valve, OFN (N₂), brazing torch, fire extinguisher (CO₂ / powder), signage ("zone d'intervention — flamme interdite"), ventilation fan (ATEX), PPE.

## Correct procedure
1. **Risk assessment (12.04)**: room volume & ventilation, ignition sources within ~ 3 m ⚠ (sockets, switches, radiator), customers → define the work zone, signage, extinguisher at hand (12.05, 12.13).
2. Equipment: detector on and running continuously at floor level (R290 is heavier than air), ATEX ventilation fan, tools rated for flammables (12.02).
3. Isolate electrically, unplug the case **outside** the zone if possible.
4. Access: fit line-tap valves on process tubes if no service port.
5. **Recover (12.06)** into the HC cylinder with an A3-rated unit ⚠ (some guidance allows controlled venting outside for small HC charges — the game teaches recovery, consistent with 12.06).
6. **Purge with OFN**: pressurise with N₂, release (outside), repeat — removes residual propane before any brazing. Confirm with the detector at the opening (0 % LFL).
7. Open/replace the compressor (12.07): cut tubes (pipe cutter), braze **only after** purge, with N₂ sweep; or use approved mechanical (Lokring-type) joints.
8. Pressure test with N₂ (12.08), vacuum test (12.09).
9. **Charge by weight, precise to ± 5 g (12.10)** — small charges, overcharge is both a performance and a safety issue. Liquid phase.
10. Direct leak check with an HC detector (12.11). Close process tubes (pinch/braze cap) if line-taps must not remain.
11. Report (12.12), label maintained (12.01).

## Charge-limit mini-challenge (1.08, 12.03)
Calculator questions using EN 378 / IEC values ⚠ (verify numbers at implementation):
- LFL R290 = 0.038 kg/m³ → m₁ = 4 m³ × LFL = 0.152 kg, m₂ = 26 m³ × LFL = 0.988 kg, m₃ = 130 m³ × LFL = 4.94 kg.
- Comfort AC (IEC 60335-2-40): m_max = 2.5 × LFL^(5/4) × h₀ × √A (h₀ by mounting: floor 0.6, wall 1.8, window 1.0, ceiling 2.2 m).
- "Can this 0.4 kg R290 split go in a 12 m² bedroom, wall mounted?" → compute and decide.

## Mistakes
| id | Sev. | Trigger | Feedback |
|---|---|---|---|
| ignition-source | critical | Work with active ignition source in zone | Explosion risk |
| flame-before-purge | critical | Torch before OFN purge confirmed 0 % LFL | Fire / explosion |
| no-detector | critical | Open circuit without running detector | |
| non-atex-tools | major | Standard recovery unit / vacuum pump with sparking switch | |
| hc-into-hfc-cylinder | critical | Recover R290 into an HFC cylinder | Flammable mix, mislabelled |
| charge-error | major | Charge ≠ label ± 5 g | |
| detector-high | minor | Detector held at head height | Propane accumulates at floor level |

## Physics
Static model + **concentration field** around the opening (simple diffusion with floor accumulation); ignition if concentration > LFL at an active source → game over animation (no gore, a flash and the debrief).

## Success
No critical mistake, charge ± 5 g, report complete.
